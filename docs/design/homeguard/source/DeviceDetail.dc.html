What to do

Update the camera to v3.1.2

This fixes both problems below. Your recordings and settings stay as they are.

  1. Open the Reolink app on your phone.
  2. Tap the camera, then Settings → Device info → Firmware.
  3. Tap Check for update and wait until the camera restarts (about 5 minutes).
  4. Come back here and tap I've updated it.
I've updated it Open the vendor's update page

Can't update right now? Unplug the camera or move it to your guest Wi‑Fi until you can.
